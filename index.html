* Time = along-surface distance ÷ pace, where the pace is the flat-ground speed above adjusted for each step's gradient by Tobler's hiking function (developed for hikers on Earth, not validated for spacesuited Mars EVA). Terrain cost = along-surface distance × the experimental slope penalty (1.0 / 1.5 / 3.0 / 8.0), in metre-equivalents. Slope exposure uses the average slope of each step. Elevation gain adds up every upward step on the 20 m DEM, so small-scale noise overstates it slightly. Penalties, pace and the slope limit are assumptions. No route is guaranteed safe.
